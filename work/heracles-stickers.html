Labours of Heracles Stickers

In my mind Heracles always appeared as a strong hero that was infallible. But what if he was really someone just trying to get along in life but kept running into trouble? I created a comical sticker set to find a way to express my passion for greek mythology and something people can relate to with humour. I drew the original sketches by hand and then placed them into illustrator. I stuck with a straight lined jagged approach to keep a similar style among all fo them.
